# NetworkDiagramStudio preview.54

## A tidier, clearer editor

- **One place for each job:**
  - The header has a single **Export…** button for every format.
  - Quick exports of the drawing (**Print / PDF**, **Visio**, **Page SVG**) sit together beside the drawing tools.
- **Clearer names:**
  - **Page options** is now **Paper & print**: paper size, orientation, page lock, margin, printing and page numbers. Its save button is **Apply paper settings**.
  - **Pages & layout** is now **Layout & content**: page names, sizes, text, information panel and arrangement.
  - **+ Add** is now **Add connection**, **Reports** is **Switchport reports**, and the sidebar's **Add** is **Add device**.
- **Edit toolbar:**
  - Tools are grouped and separated: history, selection, align and group, resize, snapping, then page and arrangement.
  - Labels are plain words: **Properties…**, **Deselect**, **Smaller**, **Larger**, **Set size…**, **Set spacing…** and **Snap settings…**.
  - **Resize** chooses whether Smaller and Larger change the **Objects** or the **Spacing between them**.
  - **Snap label to line** only appears when it applies.
- **More room for the drawing:** on wide windows, **Hide project** collapses the project, import and device panel. The toolbar then fits on one row and the canvas gets about 250 px wider. Your browser remembers the choice.
- **Getting started:** the welcome card offers **Import files**, **Open project** and **Open sample site**.
- **Shorter help:** the edit-mode help line is now short enough to read at a glance.

Save your project before updating.
