# NetworkDiagramStudio preview.56

## Typing is instant again

- Typing in **Site name** no longer freezes the app. The heading updates as you type, and the drawing rebuilds once when you pause, press Enter or click elsewhere.
- One rename is now one Undo step.

## Larger, tidier wireless pages

- **Bigger APs:** when a sheet has room, the AP cards grow to fill it while the switch port numbers stay readable. On a fictional site like yours (8-member and 5-member stacks):

  | Paper | AP text, sheet 1 | AP text, sheet 2 |
  |---|---|---|
  | Letter | 4.3 → 5.1 pt | 5.2 → 5.2 pt |
  | A4 | 4.2 → 4.5 pt | 5.1 → 5.1 pt |
  | Tabloid | 5.9 → 7.5 pt | 7.3 → 7.9 pt |

- **Stack members sit closer together:** cables leaving the gap between two members toward opposite sides now share rows instead of stepping down one by one.
- **No more lines across a switch:** a cable no longer runs sideways across a switch's faceplate between its port rows.
- **Fewer tangles and whole names:** each sheet keeps no more cable crossings than APs, and AP names stay on one line.
- **Dynamic pages look like the paper sheets:** the same compact arrangement around the whole stack, with the larger APs. Moving an AP keeps everything else where it is.

## Port numbers on the network page

- Switch and firewall faceplates on the **Core Network** page now show their port numbers, like the wireless pages.
- Ports with a cable on the page keep their port label instead, so cables are drawn exactly as before.
- Turn the numbers off in **Layout & content → Show port numbers on network pages**.

## A calmer, easier editor

- **Edit drawing is now the first switch in the drawing toolbar,** right after the page title, in the same place at every window size. It turns solid with a green switch while you are editing.
- The toolbars are lighter: quiet buttons grouped in neat clusters, with **Export…** as the one highlighted action.
- The edit tools take less room, with small icons for Undo, Redo, Snap settings and the Place beside directions (hover for their names).
- Device rows in the sidebar are shorter, with small ↑ ↓ ✕ buttons on the same line.

Save your project before updating.
