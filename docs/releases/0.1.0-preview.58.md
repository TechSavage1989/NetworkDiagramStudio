# NetworkDiagramStudio preview.58

## Place each device's name where you want it

- **One device:** turn on **Edit drawing**, click the device, choose **Properties…** and pick **Name position** and **Details position**: above or below, left or right. The same choices are in the device's details (click its name in the device list).
- **Every device:** **Layout & content** now shows **Device name position** and **Device details position** while you are on a network page, not only under Drawing defaults.
- A name or detail line you had dragged moves to the corner you pick. Undo puts it back.

## Port numbers on every device that has ports

- On the **Core Network** page, every device drawn with real ports is now numbered: routers, patch panels and other recognized hardware, not only switches and firewalls. Ports with a cable keep their port name.
- Devices drawn as plain symbols have no ports to number.
- Turn the numbers off in **Layout & content → Show port numbers on network pages**.

Save your project before updating.
