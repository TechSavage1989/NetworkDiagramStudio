# NetworkDiagramStudio preview.52

## Wireless pages: every access point stays on its switch's sheet

On any paper size, all access points connected to one switch (or stack) are now drawn on that switch's sheet. They are no longer continued onto extra sheets.

- **Filling the page:** each wireless sheet is planned to fill the paper. APs go in rows above and below the switch, or in columns beside it, whichever prints largest, and names stay on whole lines.
- **Staggered rows:** when a switch has many APs, they use two staggered rows above and below it. Each cable drops straight between the cards nearer the switch, so cables do not run through captions and the left-to-right port order is kept.
- **Readable trade-off:** on Letter, a switch with 30–32 access points prints its AP details at about 5.4–5.8 pt and its port numbers at about 4.8–5.2 pt. Tabloid and A3 print about 8 pt. The page note says this, and how to get larger text: choose larger paper, or Page options → Wireless paper layout → Continue on sheets for 8 pt continuation sheets.
- **Speed:** planning a dense site takes a few seconds per paper size (1–9 s for the fictional 60-AP test site).

## Port inventory has its own page group

The Pages sidebar now lists Port Inventory tables in their own "Port inventory" group, separate from Port maps. Paper settings for port maps and inventory stay shared; Pages & layout calls that group "Port maps and inventory".

## Saved projects

Automatic wireless layouts saved by preview.49, preview.50 or preview.51 are re-planned to one sheet per switch when opened. Pages whose access points, labels or cables you moved by hand keep their saved layout. Keep complete layout, Continue on sheets, Preserve mode and paper locks are unchanged.

Save your project before updating.
