# NetworkDiagramStudio preview.48

## More reliable settings previews

Live preview now keeps your selected state, using an adaptive delay after slow layout calculations rather than turning itself off. Resize the controls area, drag with the left or middle mouse button to pan even a fitted page, or use the mouse wheel to zoom around the pointer.

Actual size and Calibrate screen are now available in the three live-preview settings dialogs, sharing the main drawing calibration. Physical scale stays fixed when the pane changes size. Fit or manual zoom leaves Actual size. Check calibration with a ruler or paper after changing displays. These are inspection controls, not changes to exported page geometry.

Apply saves the draft once; Cancel, Close and Escape leave the project unchanged. Large synchronous layout calculations can still briefly delay interaction.

## Make better use of wireless side space

Use side space now compares mixed side, top and bottom AP arrangements as well as side columns. Changing AP size or re-applying placement recalculates automatic wireless frames while retaining the paper lock. Saved side layouts keep their arrangement after reopening or moving an AP. Deliberately authored geometry remains protected.

For an older automatically arranged page, open Pages & layout, select the wireless page, choose Use side space and apply. Very dense single-sheet diagrams may still require smaller text; choose larger paper or readable continuation pages rather than expecting every crowded Letter diagram to meet an 8 pt target.

Save your project before updating.
