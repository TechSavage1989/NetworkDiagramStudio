# NetworkDiagramStudio preview.51

## Wireless pages on Letter and other paper: readable, efficient, no more one-AP pages

Choosing Letter (or another paper size) for a dense wireless page no longer splits the site into dozens of nearly empty sheets. Preview.50 shrank an enlarged drawing onto the paper and then kept halving sheets until each held one access point, which made nothing larger. Automatic wireless pages are now planned at the size they print:

- AP names, models, ports, addresses and MAC lines are planned at their real printed size (at least 8 pt on Letter for typical sites), and names and details break between words instead of mid-address.
- Each sheet shows the stack members its access points connect to, so a busy member is not crowded out by unrelated members. The complete stack is kept when that needs no more sheets.
- The stack is drawn as narrow as its port numbers allow, and access points use the rows above and below it and the space beside it, whichever prints larger. An explicit Use side space choice is honored.
- Continuation sheets are used only when needed, with balanced sheets that keep each stack member's and each access point's links together. Every sheet keeps the selected paper size, and every access point and link appears exactly once.
- Cables never cross access-point captions, and a sheet whose cables would tangle uses one more sheet only when that removes the tangle.

A fictional site with an 8-member stack (32 APs) and a 5-member stack (28 APs) now prints as 4 Letter sheets with AP text from 8.4 to 9.4 pt and readable port numbers, instead of 60 sheets at 5.4 pt.

Saved projects: automatic wireless layouts saved by preview.49 or preview.50 are re-planned when opened. Pages with deliberately moved access points, labels or cables, Keep complete layout choices, Preserve mode and paper locks keep their saved layout. A locked page still accepts deliberate manual moves past its edge and reports the print clipping.

Very small paper (A5) cannot hold 8 pt AP cards beside a 48-port switch; those sheets report the smaller size.

Save your project before updating.
