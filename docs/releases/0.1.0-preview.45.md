# NetworkDiagramStudio 0.1.0-preview.45

## Calibrated actual-size screen view

- Choose physical paper in Page options and click **Actual size** to check printed label readability at calibrated 1:1 size. Physical Letter port-map sheets also work without a separate paper override.
- **Calibrate screen** lets you hold a 100 mm ruler, an 85.60 mm standard card, or the short edge of A4/Letter paper against the display and adjust a matching reference line. Save the calibration, then hold your matching sheet over the page to verify its edges.
- Sheets larger than the viewport retain their actual size. Hold Space and drag, or use the middle mouse button, to pan. Fit page and manual zoom leave actual-size mode.
- Calibration persists locally in this browser, independently of projects and exports, and can be reset. Detectable browser zoom, display-resolution and scaling changes invalidate the actual-size claim and prompt recalibration.

Screen resolution alone cannot establish physical size. Recheck against a ruler or paper after changing monitors, browser zoom, OS scaling or resolution: some monitor changes cannot be detected. Calibration is not an absolute size guarantee across displays. Use matching paper and 100% printer scaling for a matching print. Dynamic canvases need a physical sheet choice first.

This changes screen viewing only, not diagram geometry, paper settings or export dimensions. Save open projects before updating. Previous releases remain available.

## Multi-object resizing

- Fixed generated link captions accidentally becoming pinned during equipment/group resizing, which could reject the operation with “This change overlaps a link label.” Automatic captions now follow the rerouted cables.
- Explicit manual label placement, custom text/styles, connected ports and Undo/redo remain preserved. Existing collision checks still protect genuinely obstructed layouts.

## Readable paper panels and wireless layouts

- Docked information panels and wireless link-color keys retain readable paper-sized text instead of shrinking with the diagram. The model fits the remaining space; explicit manual panel geometry remains authoritative.
- Existing frozen layouts are preserved. If an older saved frame needs the new panel fit, follow the visible prompt to **Page options → Save page options** to deliberately recalculate it. Dense panel content reports clipping instead of silently becoming unreadable; choose larger paper or fewer sections when needed.
- Improved upper AP cable ordering within compatible switch-member gaps. The five-member/28-AP regression drops from 42 to 16 crossings without added route length or moved APs. Remaining crossings are not claimed eliminated; physical sockets, saved bends and safety clearances remain constraints.

## Interactive HTML inspection

- Hover or focus devices, ports and cables to inspect labels and highlight related connections. Click or press Enter to pin details; Escape clears them.
- Pages are parsed lazily, connection lookups are cached, and pan/zoom updates are coalesced per animation frame to reduce redundant work.
- The export stays self-contained, offline and read-only, with no project evidence or raw configuration records embedded.
