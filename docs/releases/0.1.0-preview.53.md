# NetworkDiagramStudio preview.53

## Wireless sheets show the whole stack, with APs all around it

- **Whole stack:** each switch's wireless sheet now shows the whole stack, not just the member its APs plug into. It falls back to members only when the whole stack would print more than 30% smaller.
- **Larger text instead:** to trade the whole stack for larger text, choose **Page options → Wireless stack view → Only stack members with AP links**.
- **APs all around:** access points are placed above, below and beside the stack, whichever prints largest with the fewest crossing cables. Cards stay on whole lines.
- **Use side space:** this choice still puts APs beside the stack, but now also uses the rows above and below instead of forcing every AP into side columns.
- **Upright port numbers:** switch port numbers on wireless sheets are never drawn sideways. The switch is drawn wide enough for upright numbers.
- **Text size trade-off:** on Letter, a 32-AP member of an 8-member stack prints AP details at about 4.2 pt with the whole stack, or about 5.6 pt with members only. Tabloid and A3 print larger, and the page note on each sheet states the size.

## Page margin setting

**Page options → Page margin (mm)** sets the blank border around every drawing page and port map. Leave it empty for the automatic margin.

## Faster changes

- **Edits elsewhere:** wireless sheets are reused when their switch and its access points have not changed. Renaming a core switch or moving a core device no longer re-plans them: a rebuild of a dense fictional 60-AP site took about 0.6 s instead of about 3.5 s.
- **AP edits:** an AP change re-plans only its own switch.
- **Re-opening and undo/redo:** remembered layouts make these faster too.
- **Still slower:** changing a text or line-jump style, or choosing a new paper size, still plans the wireless sheets again (a few seconds for that site).

## Saved projects

Automatic wireless layouts are re-planned when opened. Pages with access points, labels or cables you moved by hand keep their saved layout. Paper locks now also record the stack view and plan, so locked sheets reopen exactly.
