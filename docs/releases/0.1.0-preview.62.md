# NetworkDiagramStudio preview.62

## Site map

- **A new Site Map page**, after the network page, gives a one-page overview of the site's hardware and how it connects, without port details:
  - one card per device, stack, or switch's group of access points;
  - arranged from the WAN handoffs and firewalls through core and access switches to access points, servers and other devices;
  - one line per pair of connected devices, coloured by connection type and labelled with the link count and speed (for example "2 × 10 Gbps LAG").
- **Site map options** (shown on the site map) choose:
  - what each card shows: device type, model, platform, management IP, port count, VLAN count, serial number, MAC address and notes;
  - whether access points and other devices are grouped, shown one by one or left out;
  - the connection captions;
  - the direction: automatic, top to bottom, or left to right;
  - whether WAN handoffs and devices without a recorded connection appear.
- Devices connected only to one device above them, such as a switch's access points, sit right beside or below that device, so their lines stay short.
- In Edit drawing you can move cards and edit any text. Connections follow a moved card, and Undo reverses each change.
- On a fixed paper size the map is fitted to the sheet at the largest readable size. Exports to SVG, PDF and Visio keep the cards and their connected lines.

## Interactive HTML viewer

- **A Details panel.** Click a device to see:
  - its details;
  - every connection, with local port, the device and port at the other end, connection type and speed;
  - the pages that show it.

  Click the far device, or a page, to jump straight there.
- **Path to the network edge.** Highlights the cable path from the selected device to its firewall, router or WAN handoff.
- **Dim everything else** keeps attention on the selection.
- **Connection types.** Click one in the key (or in the panel) to highlight every cable of that type.
- **Off-page tags** lead to the page and device where the cable continues.
- **Navigation:**
  - **Back** (Alt+←) returns to the previous selection;
  - **[** and **]** change pages;
  - **/** jumps to search, which lists matching devices first;
  - links such as `#page=2&device=core1` open a device directly.

The viewer is still a single offline, read-only file with no network access. It contains only the drawing pages you export.
