# NetworkDiagramStudio preview.55

## Faster changes

- **About twice as fast:**
  - Pages rebuild about twice as fast after a change, most of all on wireless pages and paper plans.
  - In the editor, on a fictional site with an 8-member and a 5-member stack:

    | Action | Before | After |
    |---|---|---|
    | Open the project | 3.7 s | 1.8 s |
    | Choose Letter for the wireless pages | 5.5 s | 2.1 s |
    | Undo or Redo | 0.6 s | 0.2 s |

  - Renaming an access point or changing a text style or the line jump style is also about twice as fast.
- **Instant Undo and Redo:** each kind of page remembers its last few builds. Undo, Redo or going back to an earlier setting shows those pages right away instead of planning them again.
- **Same drawings:** the drawings are exactly the same as before; only the work to produce them is faster.

## Tidier start screen

- Before a drawing is open, the drawing toolbar no longer leaves an empty gap between **−** and **+**, and it fits on one row.
- The welcome card shows **Import files**, **Open project** and **Open sample site** on one row.

Save your project before updating.
