# NetworkDiagramStudio 0.1.0-preview.39

## Responsive page content

- Page options now provides **Auto-fit and optimize content** and **Preserve existing layout**.
- Auto-fit scales the complete drawing uniformly to its chosen paper proportions. Network and wireless layouts prefer arrangements that make better use of portrait and landscape pages, while retaining manually positioned content.
- Port maps with up to eight physical stack members remain on one logical sheet. Landscape pages can arrange member faceplates in balanced columns with one shared VLAN/LAG key.
- The page default, per-group and individual-page proportions remain available. Print paper stays independently selectable.

## Editor rulers

- A top and left page ruler show inches or millimeters at the current zoom.
- Click the ruler corner to switch units. Rulers track panning and zooming and stay out of exported drawings.

## Connection and label editing

- Moving a connection label manually pins its saved position, including after rebuilding a page or reopening a project.
- Select a label and choose **Snap label to line** to align it at a percentage along its cable or pick a point directly on the drawn line.
- Selecting a port, its cable, or a connection label highlights the associated objects and endpoints. Highlights are editor-only.
- Clear in-line labels are preferred where possible, with clearance checks to prevent unsafe overlaps.
- Core device movement stays outside the reserved information sidebar.

## Page navigation

- Page-group dropdowns close when clicking elsewhere, pressing Escape, selecting a page, or opening another group.

Save open projects before updating. The Windows portable executable retains its filename, NetworkDiagramStudio.exe. Use the application's verified updater or download the new preview.
