# NetworkDiagramStudio preview.66

## Network pages

- **More compact on paper.** A network page no longer falls back to one tall column just because a single port label needed room in the compact arrangement. It keeps the compact columns and makes room for the label instead, so the page fills a landscape sheet and prints much larger. On a fictional site shaped like a firewall pair, a core chassis and two stacks, text went from 2.0 pt to 3.2 pt on Letter.
- **Names and details stay against their device.** Device names and details are placed right above or below their own device (your chosen corner first, sliding along that edge past cables), at the largest size where every one of them fits there. Before, they could jump beside the device or well away from it to reach a larger size.

## Labels

- **Whole words.** Long labels wrap at spaces, then after a dash, dot or slash in names such as host names, and only then inside a word, never leaving one or two letters on their own line.
- **One piece on the cable.** A port name shown on its own cable is one run of text. Before, a wrapped name could show as "Some Interfac" … "e" along the cable.
